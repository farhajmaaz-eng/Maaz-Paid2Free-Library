#Paid2Free

Discover free and open-source alternatives to expensive software.

Paid2Free is a curated directory of software that helps you replace paid tools, reduce subscriptions, and discover useful open-source projects.

The goal isn't to collect the most links. It's to find software that's genuinely useful.

What makes a good entry?

Every recommendation should include:

- The software's name and official website
- The paid product it can replace
- Its open-source license
- Supported operating systems
- Important limitations or missing features

Our standards

- Prefer actively maintained projects.
- Link to official websites and source repositories.
- Verify licenses instead of assuming software is open source.
- Disclose important limitations.
- Avoid misleading claims about privacy, security, or functionality.
- Never link to pirated copies of proprietary software.
